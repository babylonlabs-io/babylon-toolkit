Delegated claim — depositor as claimer (devnet harness)

Dev server only. Every vault fact is read from chain; you enter the vault id and, later, the Groth16 proof.

1 · Connect wallet

Ledger: device screens you will see, in order
  1. WebHID pairing, then "Babylon Vault Testnet" open on the device
  2. Step 3 (artifacts): DERIVE_CONTEXT_HASH approval — vault-provider auth token
  3. Step 4 (WOTS): DERIVE_CONTEXT_HASH approval — WOTS keypair
  4. Step 6 (sign): DERIVE_CONTEXT_HASH approval → APPROVE_VAULT_INTENT (review terms) → sign Assert → sign depositor Payout → DERIVE_CONTEXT_HASH approval → sign claimer Payout → sign Claim → sign WronglyChallenged × (challengers × circuits)
  5. Resume after a rejection replays the whole step-6 list but skips standalone signatures already collected

2 · Read vault context from chain

3 · Download vault-provider artifacts (streams to a file you choose)

4 · Derive claimer WOTS keypair

5 · Plan (build + bind PSBTs)

6 · Sign

7 · Assemble artifacts.json

Join babe_sessions for the watchtower CLI (Node) node services/vault/scripts/join-babe-sessions.mjs --artifacts artifacts.json --bundle babylon-vault-artifacts-<txid8>.json --out artifacts.joined.json

8 · Claim

9 · Pin the Groth16 proof

10 · Assert

11 · Payout (after the Assert timelock)

Optional · WronglyChallenged (answer a ChallengeAssert)
